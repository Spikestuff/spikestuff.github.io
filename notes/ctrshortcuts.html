Papu's Pyramid

This level will show up a few times, but we'll show off the version that everyone should at least know.
At the final turn you can actually avoid this turn by having enough speed and making the jump across.

Hot Air Skyway

At the section where the later part of the track is on the other side of the wall perform a u-turn just after passing it.
Gain speed with a drift or a boost item and jump to the opposite side of the track, perform another u-turn for a quick recovery facing the correct direction, or take your time.

Note: If you don't go down the track (like the clip) you won't load more of the track risking a failed attempt on this trick.
Even if you fail this trick it's possible the game can be forgiving and can make it a valid skip by pulling you across.
